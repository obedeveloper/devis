# Devis

Create and manage professional quotes in minutes, and export them as polished PDFs. Free for freelancers and small businesses.

## What is Devis?

Devis helps you create, organize, and share business quotes — all from one simple dashboard. Whether you're a freelancer or running a small business, Devis makes it easy to stay on top of your quotes.

## Features

- **Create quotes** — Add a title, description, and choose your currency
- **Manage line items** — Break down your services with descriptions, quantities, and pricing
- **Export as PDF** — Download a single quote or batch export multiple quotes at once
- **Stay organized** — View, edit, or delete quotes anytime from your dashboard
- **Sign in with Google** — Quick and secure access with your Google account

## Getting Started

1. **Sign in** with your Google account
2. **Create a quote** by giving it a title and optional description
3. **Add line items** to detail the services or products you're quoting
4. **Export as PDF** when you're ready to share with your client
